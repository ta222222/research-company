Platform & Database

Hammer Media Research Company is a platform and a database. It exists to collect, index, and cross-reference experiments — objects and processes that move between the digital and the physical, without settling into a single fixed medium.

Entries here are logged rather than published. Where Hammer the magazine produces finished work, this is the research underneath it: material still in process, held for reference, and available for reuse.

Fields of Inquiry

The current scope spans digital and real-life objects — artifacts that exist as data and as physical things at once — alongside visual research: imaging, optics, and the mechanics of how pictures get made and seen.

A parallel line of inquiry sits in surveillance and cybersecurity: infrastructure, data exposure, and the systems that watch, log, and secure. These are treated as design subjects here, not only technical ones.

Art, Design & Access

Art exploration and design work runs through all of it — prototypes, interfaces, tools, and objects built to test an idea rather than ship a product. Some become published work; most stay in the database.

Access is limited and selective, consistent with how Hammer operates elsewhere: quiet distribution, direct contact, material released on its own schedule rather than a feed.

Listed Technology

  • Editorial platform for finished features, visual essays, and issue-based publishing across print and digital formats.

  • Capture and imaging tooling for visual research — optics experiments, documentation, and image pipelines.

  • Moving-image channel for short-form and long-form transmissions tied to Hammer research and editorial output.

  • Internal toolbox and portal stack for indexing, workflows, communications, and cross-project research access.

  • Controlled vault for research assets, credentials, and restricted materials — selective access with logged release.
